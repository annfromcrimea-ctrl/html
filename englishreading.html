CVC Short Vowels

Consonant-Vowel-Consonant. The vowel is short.

CVC Rule: When one vowel sits between two consonants, it usually says its short sound (cat, dog, sit).

/æ/ as in Apple

/ɛ/ as in Egg

/ɪ/ as in Igloo

/ɒ/ as in Octopus

/ʌ/ as in Umbrella

Doubling Consonants

When adding -ing or -ed, sometimes we double the last letter.

Rule: stop → stopped, run → running. Keep the vowel short!

Sound Blending Practice

Magic 'e'

Silent 'e' at the end makes the vowel say its name!

VCe Pattern: Consonant + Vowel + Consonant + silent e. Example: cap -> cape.

/eɪ/ Long A

/aɪ/ Long I

/oʊ/ Long O

/juː/ Long U
